## Mosey controls a motorized machine
A walking pad or treadmill is a motorized machine. Mosey starts it, changes its speed and incline, pauses it and stops it over Bluetooth, on a schedule you arm and can end at any time. That means the belt can start moving while you are standing on it, and it will change pace during a walk. You are responsible for using your pad safely:

- Read and follow your pad's own manual and safety instructions. They take precedence over anything in Mosey.
- Use the pad's safety key, clip or stop control as the manufacturer intends, and know where the pad's own stop is before you begin.
- Stand on the belt only when you are ready to walk, and step off only when it has stopped.
- Keep children, pets and anyone not walking away from the belt while it runs.
- Do not use Mosey with a pad that is damaged, unstable or not approved for the way you are using it.
- Mosey's demo pad is a simulation and moves nothing; a real pad moves.

Bluetooth can drop. If the connection is lost mid-walk, the pad continues at its last commanded speed until its own controls or its own safety features stop it. Mosey cannot stop a pad it is no longer connected to.

## Not medical advice
Mosey is not a medical device and offers no medical advice. Walking pads are not suitable for everyone. If you have a heart condition, balance or joint problems, are pregnant, or have any doubt about whether walking on a treadmill is safe for you, talk to a doctor before using Mosey. Stop immediately if you feel pain, dizziness or discomfort.
