## Which pads work?
Any pad that speaks the standard Bluetooth Fitness Machine Service (FTMS). Verified on the Sportstech sPad500. Pads without incline walk the same rounds flat.

## Mosey can't find my pad
Make sure the pad is powered on, and that your phone's Bluetooth is off: the pad's own phone app keeps hold of the connection in the background, even when closed. Mosey connects only to the pad you chose during setup.

## Trying Mosey without a pad
When no pad is found, Mosey offers a demo pad so you can see every screen. The belt readings are simulated. Choose *Forget the demo pad* from the Pad menu to go back to a real one.

## The pad started at a crawl
FTMS pads wake at 1.0 km/h and sometimes ignore the first pace command. Mosey re-asserts the walk's opening pace for the first half minute; if you see the crawl for longer, stop and begin the round again.
