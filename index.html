Will it work with my walking pad?

Very likely. Mosey talks to pads over Bluetooth the standard way, so it works with the Sportstech sPad500 we walk on every day and with twenty-odd others from KingSmith, Urevo, Mobvoi and WELLFIT. No pad yet? A demo pad is built in, so you can look around first.

What are the walks?

Fifty-two of them, three to twenty minutes, each with a reason: the mailbox, the coffee run up the hill, the trailhead twenty minutes from the office. Every walk sets its own pace and climb, and the ring draws it as you go — radius is your pace, thickness is the hill.
