The Nudge

Twenty-five on, twenty-five off. No pressure, ever.

You don't schedule a walk. You arm a rhythm. The pad eases up to pace by itself, takes you somewhere for twenty-five minutes, eases down, and rests exactly as long — then picks another walk and goes again.

Step off mid-walk and nothing is wrong; the rest just started early. Nothing counts down at you, nothing turns red, nothing keeps a streak.

The Round Book

Fifty walks with a reason.

Not workouts. The river after the laptop shuts. The long way from the station because the shortcut is exactly the problem with it. The call that's too good for the couch. Ten cities and Anywhere, five walks each.

Every walk choreographs pace and climb — the pad tilts where the story climbs. The ring draws it: radius is your pace, thickness is the hill, light is how far you've come.

Which pads

Any pad that speaks Bluetooth FTMS.

Mosey has no pad-specific code: it looks for the standard Fitness Machine Service and drives whatever answers. Verified on the Sportstech sPad500; documented on KingSmith's Z1, R3 and X218, Urevo's SpaceWalk 3S, Mobvoi, WELLFIT and every pad that ships with the FitShow app. KingSmith's older models, DeerRun and Sperax run their own protocols and won't.
