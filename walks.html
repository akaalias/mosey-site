The Round Book

Fifty-two walks, three to twenty minutes, each with a reason.

Every walk has its own length — the mailbox is three minutes, the trailhead is twenty — and is drawn as one line: the ring runs clockwise from twelve, its radius is your pace, its thickness is the hill. Ten places and Anywhere, listed the way the app's contents page lists them: by how much time you have. Open a row for the walk's reason.
