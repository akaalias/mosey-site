The Round Book

Fifty walks, each with a reason.

Every walk is twenty-five minutes and drawn as one line: the ring runs clockwise from twelve, its radius is your pace, its thickness is the hill. Ten places and Anywhere, five walks each. The pad does the pace and the tilt; you do the walking.
